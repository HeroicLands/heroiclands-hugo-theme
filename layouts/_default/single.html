{{ partial "related.html" . }} {{- /* Prev/next walk the page's own catalog, not its Hugo section (issue #48). `.PrevInSection` / `.NextInSection` mean "the neighbouring page in this directory", which was the same thing only while a page lived in a directory named for its catalog. `@heroiclands/package-build` (package-build#204) emits every content page flat under the content mount, so a consumer's whole catalog became one section and these two links walked every page of every type — reading through the afflictions silently left them.
