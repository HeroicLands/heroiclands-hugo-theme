Shared by the nested and top-level `being` list layouts. Attribute columns are DERIVED, never hardcoded: a being carries only the attributes that apply to it, and the sets genuinely differ — an animal has Scent where a person has Dexterity, and only people carry the social attributes (Comeliness, Empathy, Eloquence, Voice, Morality).
