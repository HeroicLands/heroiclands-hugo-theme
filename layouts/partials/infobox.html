{{- /* The infobox rail — every box a page declares, drawn in the order declared. **The content is not decided here.** A page's `infoboxes:` front matter carries an ordered list of boxes, each holding ordered sections, each holding ordered rows, cells, groups or entries, already labelled. This partial and the three under `infobox/` switch on a section's `layout` and a value's `kind` and on nothing else — never on a note type, never on a field name. That is what lets a field added to a type appear here with no edit. A box is a `
` disclosure, open by default: native, accessible, and no JavaScript. It is what lets several boxes stack for nothing — a note box and one box per system are three summary lines when closed, rather than three panels burying the prose. Two kinds of box, and they answer emptiness differently: - A **note box** with no section holding anything is not drawn. A heading over nothing asserts a fact that is not there. - A **system box** is always drawn, because its emptiness is the statement: a system that maps this note's type but compiled no document for it reads "Not available", which a reader meeting one page can act on. A system that does not map the type at all sends no box, and nothing is drawn.
