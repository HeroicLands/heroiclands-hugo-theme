{{- /* Affiliation infobox — floated card on pages with `type: affiliation`. An affiliation is any organized body a character can belong to: a religion or church, a school of magic, a spirit tradition, or a secular guild, bank, syndicate, house, or military unit. Which one it is comes from the top-level `subType`; there is no separate "faith" kind of page, and a religion is an affiliation and nothing more. The practices of its rites are a skill, and the powers it grants are mystical abilities — both separate documents. Fields read from frontmatter (all optional; rows silently skip when missing). The first two apply to every affiliation; the rest are meaningful only for a deity-facing one and are simply absent elsewhere. name.full — The body's name (falls back to the page title). subType — faithtradition | arcanetradition | spirittradition | …, the first three rendered as a label, the rest humanized. data.lore — The god is not a property of the affiliation; it is cited here as a `lore` note of subType `deity`, and the first one found is the row's value. data.epithet — The deity's epithet (e.g. "The Keeper of Transitions"). data.symbol — Prose description of the deity's iconographic symbol (e.g. "A jackal's head cradling a crescent moon"). img — Per-body illustration (URL fragment or full URL). If present, takes precedence over `glyph` and renders as a full-color portrait at the top of the infobox without the limestone-tablet framing. Used by the Aurèldían and Asguardian religions which have custom art. glyph — Slug of a hieroglyphic name SVG; resolves to icons/.svg on the site's asset host and renders inside a .glyph-tablet at the top of the card. Used when no `img` is provided (e.g. the Kemetían religion, which uses hieroglyphic name-glyphs rather than figural art). data.parents — The first entry is read as the parent pantheon's shortcode; rendered as a link when a page with that shortcode is found in the site. */ -}} {{- $ctx := . -}} {{- $data := dict -}}{{- with .Params.data }}{{ $data = . }}{{ end -}} {{- $name := "" -}}{{- with .Params.name.full }}{{ $name = . }}{{ end -}} {{- if not $name }}{{ $name = .Title }}{{ end -}} {{- $epithet := $data.epithet -}} {{- $symbol := $data.symbol -}} {{- $img := .Params.img -}} {{- $glyph := .Params.glyph -}} {{- /* The deity is a `lore` note cited from `data.lore`, not a property of the affiliation — an affiliation can outlive belief in the god, and one god may be venerated by several.
